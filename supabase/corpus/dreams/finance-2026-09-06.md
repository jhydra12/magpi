## What changed

The unit cost model for the Fold S1 device was updated to version 11, confirming a pilot volume unit cost of $1,140 per device. Recent changes included correcting assembly labour to the pilot line rate, switching the outer layer to a Meniscus-C polymer (down $7/unit but with a new $19 scrap allowance due to unproven yield), and factoring in September freight rates. These updates brought clarity to each line item, added vendor quote references, and established a direct linkage between the workbook and the signed board pack summary. Work on accessories also continued, refining attach rates and costs for the folio case and desk stand.

## What was decided

- The launch price is set at $1,899 per device, yielding approximately 40% gross margin before downstream costs, per Jane Okonkwo’s decision.
- All unit cost and pricing work should reflect $1,140 as device cost and $1,899 as the customer-facing launch price.
- The cost model workbook is the canonical reference; if the summary drifts, the workbook takes precedence.
- Desk stand accessory will be offered in Chalk finish only to control tooling costs.
- The accessory range will be limited to two products (folio case and desk stand) with no immediate plan for expansion.

## What is unresolved

- The warranty reserve of $11 per device is a placeholder; a more robust return rate model is needed.
- The lamination scrap allowance ($19/unit) is an estimate. Actual yield data from the pilot build is required to update this assumption.
- Second source quotes for the hinge assembly and a volume-tier quote for the polymer layer are pending.
- Real-world attach rates for accessories are based on public data; carrier-sourced or channel-specific data is still needed.
- A decision is needed on whether accessories should be included in official board reporting, given their small share of revenue.
- The workbook maintains open questions about relabeling certain cost lines for clarity in future revisions.
