## What changed

The team shifted the outer layer material from glass to polymer just before the tooling commitment, resulting in a net capex saving of $35,000 and a small $18,000 engineering cost for abandoned work. The lamination fixture now costs $214,000 instead of $267,000. Cash burn in August ran higher than average, mainly due to timing for test house deposit and certification fees, and some one-off costs for the material switch and cosmetic re-measurement. Current cash runway stands at 14 months at operating burn.

## What was decided

The polymer fixture will replace the glass one, and the second fixture (needed for two-shift operations at higher launch volume) will be reconsidered after the pilot build yields are known. The team will not try to reduce unit cost this quarter as volume itself will drive key cost improvements. Committed to pilot build material purchases in October, even ahead of pilot build results, because of lead times. The launch price is set at $1,899, with a 40% gross margin before channel costs.

## What is unresolved

The timing and approach for raising funds to cover the first production build remains unsettled; input from the board is being sought. Approval is needed for committing funds for pilot build materials before yield results are in. The schedule for purchasing a second lamination fixture depends on the actual pilot yield, which is still to be determined. The team is also seeking introductions to investors experienced in this specific funding gap.
