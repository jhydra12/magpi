## What changed

The Fold S1 phone will ship in four finishes (Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember) rather than the six originally in review. The change to the Meniscus-C polymer outer layer on 27 August caused two finishes, Tide (pale blue) and Rust (oxide red), to be cut: Tide failed to match its colour standard with the new coating, and Rust lost its slot for a necessary second anodising pass due to the die reorder timeline. The team also locked down the first quarter build plan at 180,000 units for nine markets (four in tier 1, five in tier 2), with manufacturing runs scheduled and preliminary freight/logistics for the pilot build confirmed, including secured visas and shipment plans for key fixtures. Certification lab slots and device allocation for regulatory tests were also updated and confirmed.

## What was decided

It was reaffirmed that only four finishes will ship due to both manufacturing capacity and the unsolvable issues introduced by the material change. The four finishes and their production allocations are fixed, with reasons for dropping Tide and Rust clearly documented for internal use and external messaging. The build schedule, volume per market, and run calendar are established, with clear priorities for market launches and supply chain logistics. The team decided on specific units and processes for certification testing, with device b14 designated as a spare for EMC testing. It was also decided (and communicated) to not promise any potential fifth finish or name the cut colours publicly.

## What is unresolved

Several points remain open:
- The firm order for the film vendor must be placed by 30 September.
- A second source is still needed for the panel 4 flex connector.
- The agreement with the Sydney hub is unsigned.
- The Netherlands launch lacks a carrier, and it isn’t clear who will close that gap.
- Tier 3 markets (Singapore, UAE) have no committed dates or regulatory submission owner.
- Colourway names still require translation for packaging.
- There is an outstanding question about whether Ember’s planned volume matches available anodising capacity.
- No decision has been made about a possible fifth finish; it is not to be discussed externally.
