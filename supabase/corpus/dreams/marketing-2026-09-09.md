## What changed

The team clarified the readiness state for each of the five tier one markets, identifying key blockers for launch. The packaging plan, including colourway names (Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember), was signed off and scheduled to meet a tight print slot. The hero photography plan now has a target to receive two cosmetically perfect units by 26 September. Both carriers' launch dates were recorded in writing, resolving exclusivity language via a factual retail date difference. Internal component names (Meniscus, Bellows, Ori) were officially kept internal and stripped from public-facing materials.

## What was decided

- Colourway names are finalized and packaging art must be delivered in time for the first print slot, with a fallback plain sleeve design prepared.
- Internal component and state names will not be used publicly; all public references will use generic descriptions.
- Carrier A will be described as "first to offer," Carrier B as "launch partner," with no exclusivity implied.
- Delivery dates for carrier-branded assets are set, with a 20 October deadline for anything live on launch day.
- Canada is handled as a tier 2 market on a tier 1 partner schedule, with clear separation of dates.
- No part shipments for a single finish will be accepted across two delivery dates.

## What is unresolved

- Korea's launch tranche is conditional on passing national type approval the week of 5 October; there is no contingency for a failed approval.
- The first cosmetically perfect production units are not yet available, delaying the review and photo programme.
- The packaging process depends on receipt of the final dieline from the moulder and proof approval; if the matte print proof is unsatisfactory, the team will revert to a plainer packaging fallback.
- The possibility remains that the demand on the small team will exceed capacity if multiple market agencies require answers concurrently.
- It is still to be checked if supplier documents contain internal codenames ("Ori" in launch post draft; Dana to confirm for supplier files). 
- Review units for US press are still pending due to unavailability of hardware with final production cosmetics.
