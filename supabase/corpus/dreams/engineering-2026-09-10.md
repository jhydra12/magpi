## What changed

The Shenzhen pilot line drawing package is nearly complete; mechanical drawings (now at revision E), hinge fixture, new ground contact, hinge cover, and assembly steps are all finalized and uploaded, except for the end of line test script, which is pending a build configuration. The folio case problem at hinge 3 was confirmed to be a design misfit, with the vendor agreeing to redraw the spine as three flexures and taper the section to fit. Firmware rc2 was released with improvements to handoff performance, sensor warnings, and naming consistency. For the desk stand, changes were made to the magnet boss design to correct potential assembly errors and cosmetic issues. Extensive drop and surface finish laboratory testing was completed and uploaded, with observations on packaging durability, accessory behavior, and panel finishes. The test house readiness checklist was submitted for certification testing, with several items still incomplete.

## What was decided

The drawing package will proceed without the end of line script, which will be delivered later once ENG-259's build config is available. The folio case design will move to three independent flexures in the spine and a tapered section per ENV-4; all four finishes depend on a single geometry change, so one fix will be applied to all. Identified firmware improvements are now included in rc2, but no further changes will go in before lab testing, except for urgent fixes. The decision was made to resolve stand assembly issues by thickening the magnet boss (increasing height by 1.1 mm), not moving the magnet, and to key the pocket to prevent polarity errors during assembly—a functional change to ensure it isn’t value engineered out. Monitoring of gloss and finish, especially on EMBER, will be done per production lot, since the margin is tight but not out-of-spec. Accession of omitted checklist items—mechanical configurations, units, regulatory signatures, and supporting documentation—will be required before certification proceeds.

## What is unresolved

The Shenzhen drawing package's end of line test script remains blocked on ENG-259’s build config, and will be sent after freeze. The new folio case sample is under development, with open questions regarding long-term surface contact and observer findings on finish panels—particularly the robustness of EMBER's gloss and the handling of any issues in future lots. Some test house checklist items are incomplete: FCC ID filing, documentation and jigs for LAPTOP and TENT states, shipment of functional units and spares, battery safety and voice results, regulatory sign-offs, and ambient confirmation for thermal testing. Retesting of desk stand performance on production tooling is also pending.
