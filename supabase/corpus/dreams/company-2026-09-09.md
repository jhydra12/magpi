## What changed

The timing for the company all-hands meeting was moved to accommodate team members in Shenzhen, ensuring it doesn't occur at 2am local time for them. The team finalized and restricted the accessory lineup to two items: a folio case (in four finishes) and a desk stand (Chalk finish only). Progress was made on certification readiness, particularly folding state tests, and a thermal issue was identified and addressed with a firmware update. There was also hardware progress: hinge cycle testing is on track, and polymer supply is stable with ongoing vendor discussions for a second source. The glossary was updated as a living document.

## What was decided

- The all-hands meeting will be held at a time suitable for those in Shenzhen for the next two weeks.
- Only two accessories will be launched, with all other potential accessories ruled out.
- Folio case comes in four finishes; the desk stand is Chalk only.
- No screen protector will be sold, due to negative impact on device use and user experience.
- The desk stand has a single detection profile and is not adjustable.
- Test house booking for certification on the 14th is fixed; all other plans revolve around it.
- Charge taper firmware fix will be included in version 0.7.8 and all units sent to the lab will have it.
- Internal component names (Meniscus, Bellows, Ori) will remain internal and not be used in marketing.

## What is unresolved

- The second (improved) folio case sample, which must fit in all folding states, is awaited.
- Ensuring the folio case does not interfere with the outer display layer requires further validation.
- Testing the desk stand on final production tooling is pending.
- A decision is outstanding on whether the desk stand will be available at launch or later.
- RF pre-scanning for LAPTOP and TENT folding states is still to be completed.
- The BOOK fold state requires further margin improvement for certification.
- The shipment of golden units for lab testing is not yet accomplished.
- The search for a second polymer source is ongoing, with vendor conversations yet to conclude.
- Desk stand detection software and case fit issues are still being worked on.
