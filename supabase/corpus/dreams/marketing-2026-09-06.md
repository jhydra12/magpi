## What changed

The main updates are working notes and early recommendations on colourway allocations for a new foldable device, based on panel results from recent research. The team is on the second draft of the crosstab and table volumes. Insights were clarified regarding the meaning and implications of market and segment findings for finishes like Moss and Ember, including their impact across markets and buyer types. Early allocation numbers have been suggested for regions based on research, and there are contextual additions explaining why Korea and Japan are responsible for forty percent of the initial production plan.

## What was decided

No binding decisions were made. Several clarifications and positions were established:
- Moss is a market-dependent finish with very high uptake in Korea and Japan, shaping the regional product mix.
- Ember’s appeal is segment-led and should remain available, as its intent-to-purchase signal is strong despite lower overall share.
- Desk stand allocation for the United States should be heavier due to alignment of segment, finish, and market demand.
- Folio case colours should simply follow device finish by market.
- The final set of four finishes and their names (Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember) are to be locked for packaging; the process around this is underway.
- The two other finishes discussed previously were not killed by the research, as they never reached the panel.
- The structure and content of the upcoming press briefing was outlined, with embargo and claims clearance processes highlighted.
- Korea and Japan's heavy weighting in the launch plan was explained based on established demand, category familiarity, and carrier commitment.

## What is unresolved

Several open questions and tasks remain:
- Whether the highly uneven regional mix is feasible at the pilot manufacturing volumes — this is for Dana to resolve.
- Whether Moss’s high popularity in Korea and Japan should translate to market-specific marketing materials, especially photography.
- How carrier preferences in certain markets might disrupt or affect planned colourway allocations.
- The impact of certification timing, especially in Korea, on launch volumes and allocation plans.
- Confirmation of panel results with a second Korean wave after receiving production units, and more reliable data from Germany.
- Final clearance of facts and inclusion of the pricing line for the press deck.
- Some process items for the press briefing (review unit logging, slide claim clearance) are pending.
