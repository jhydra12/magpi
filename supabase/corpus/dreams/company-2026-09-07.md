## What changed

The second tray revision was approved after initial resistance, due to the need to ensure the tray fits properly and doesn’t cause failures during the packing station test. There were updates on the accessory supply chain: the case is on its second vendor sample (after the first failed), and a mechanical change was introduced in the stand design to improve the magnet pocket. The paper band around the cable was iterated on and now meets requirements.

## What was decided

Packaging (sleeves, trays, wallets, bands) must physically arrive at the Shenzhen factory before the pilot run. The four colourway names—Ink, Chalk, Moss, and Ember—were finalized, thus clearing the gate for sleeve production. The decision to use moulded fibre trays with no plastic has been reaffirmed, rejecting requests for a plastic cable bag.

## What is unresolved

It remains unknown whether the second case sample will be correct; its outcome will determine if cases are ready for launch. The stand’s final design is still pending the mechanical modification, and tooling will only be cut once this is finalized. Maya needs to confirm the actual date sleeve files are sent, and Sam needs to communicate the case sample result promptly. Ongoing vigilance is required to ensure no late changes to the colourway names, as any such change would cause significant disruption.
