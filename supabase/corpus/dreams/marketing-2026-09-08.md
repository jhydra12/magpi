## What changed

- The outer layer material of the Fold S1 was changed by engineering at the end of August, moving away from glass to a polymer due to durability concerns.
- Seven of nine launch-related materials (including decks, website, sizzle script, carrier documents, FAQs) have been updated to remove references to glass; the website spec table and the sizzle script are still in progress.
- The launch demo script was iterated to cut technical panel state names from the spoken script and contains detailed instructions and contingency planning.
- The launch plan is confirmed for 4 November 2026 at 09:00 Eastern, with embargo and communication rules set. Two carriers ("Alder" and "Birch") are signed using codenames.
- Retail display unit fixture decisions are made (e.g., display in tablet mode, center tethering, ink colorway), and the swap/spare rotation approach is set.

## What was decided

- The messaging will focus on the durability and improvement: the polymer outer layer was chosen because it survives over 180,000 folds, unlike glass. However, the narrative will avoid making "the glass cracked" the headline.
- The demo will use the last rehearsed build, not the newest. If the device crashes during the demo, the procedure is to restart discreetly without explanation.
- Carrier announcements will be coordinated with launch timing; Alder will announce at embargo lift, Birch's timing is still under discussion.
- Display units will rest in tablet state with a center panel tether, and spares will be rotated in on a schedule. Ink is the primary display colorway.
- Accessories (folio case and desk stand) will be present on the retail fixture, but whether the desk stand goes on counter at launch depends on on-device detection reliability.

## What is unresolved

- Final updates are still pending for the website spec table (awaiting hardness number) and the rewritten sizzle script.
- The message content for the 0:00 demo event is undecided; message should be real, not staged.
- Demo rehearsals with Priya and confirmation that the demo unit has correct production cosmetics are pending.
- Birch carrier still wants a 24-hour head start on their announcement following launch; this is being negotiated.
- Product photography is blocked pending a final production unit with polymer panel and full finish.
- Whether any media briefings happen earlier than six weeks out has not yet been decided.
- Final unit counts for retail display from carriers, fixture vendor drawings, and a written store staff swap procedure are still outstanding.
- Decision on whether the desk stand will be displayed at launch or delayed is open, pending software reliability.
