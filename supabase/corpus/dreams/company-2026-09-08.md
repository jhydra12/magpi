### What changed

The markets section for the September board update now includes detailed market entry plans and launch sequencing, which was specifically requested after the August update lacked this detail. The launch strategy has been clarified into three tiers, with launch countries, their ordering and timing specified for the first time. Forecasted unit numbers are shared and broken down by country, input sources, and estimated demand per market and finish. Two color finishes (Tide and Rust) were dropped due to technical reasons linked to the new coating process.

### What was decided

- Product will launch in 11 markets in three tiers, with five markets (Tier 1) at launch, followed by four markets (Tier 2) six weeks later and the last two (Tier 3) as a 2027 objective without a set date.
- Forecast numbers are set at 180,000 units for Tiers 1 and 2 in Q1, with detailed breakdowns.
- Two carrier partners have committed to 71,000 of those units, pending countersignature by the end of September.
- Four finishes (Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember) will ship, after cutting Tide and Rust.
- No additional requests will be made of the board this month; open issues are seen as operational.

### What is unresolved

- There is no committed date or regulatory lead for the Singapore and UAE launches in Tier 3.
- Both carrier agreements (making up almost 40% of the plan) are not yet finalized and must be countersigned by September 30.
- Major risks remain: the Korean national approval is dependent on a single October lab week, and production delays for key demonstration hardware persist.
- Staffing is stretched thin with only four people covering nine markets in the initial launch phases.
- Vendor dependency for the outer layer continues to be a key underlying risk.
- The decision on whether launching in five Tier 1 markets at once was optimal versus focusing on fewer markets remains open and is to be revisited in October.
