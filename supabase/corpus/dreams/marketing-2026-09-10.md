## What changed

- The regional demand model for the Fold S1 was updated to build 4, which now includes only four colourways (Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember). Tide and Rust were cut after a review, and the overall volume plan is constrained to 180,000 units for the first quarter starting 4 November 2026.
- Carrier sell-in commitments were received verbally from both Alder and Birch, totaling 71,000 units of the planned 180,000. The Birch sell-in sheet and alder commitments by finish/market were uploaded, clarifying the detailed market, tranche, replenishment, and finish splits.
- Colourway names were locked for packaging with signed, written approval (Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember), and Ink was reconfirmed as the hero finish in global assets, with Moss used as the local hero for Korea and Japan.
- Creative direction for Commuter and Creator segments was refined: the Commuter asset will now open on the shut device doing something realistic (camera preview), unfold once to tablet, and only quietly reference the desk state at the end. The desk state film was reframed as a Creator segment film requiring a Moss unit, reflecting regional market weighting and segment preference.
- Packaging proofs highlighted a problem with sleeve print stock that made the fold line art appear as a scratch; new proofing on the correct stock was requested, with a fallback to plain sleeves if unresolved.
- The copy, rationale, and naming policy for the four finishes were finalized, including the public narrative for why only four colours shipped.

## What was decided

- The four colourways (Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember) are locked as public names and will be printed unchanged in all markets (English for now; translation checks ongoing in KR, JP, DE).
- The exact one-line copy for each finish, the reasoning for four not six colours (manufacturing, not taste), and communication policy (never name cut finishes publicly, no alluding to colour shifts) are finalized for all launch-facing materials.
- The creative approach for the Commuter segment will begin with the shut device (camera preview), open once to tablet, and only show the desk state briefly and wordlessly at the end.
- The packaging art cannot be released until the new proof on the correct sleeve stock is reviewed; fold line art will be dropped if unresolved.
- The packaging sleeve QR code points to a non-existent setup page—this is not a print blocker, but creating the page is assigned and open.
- Carrier partners will not have exclusive finishes, and retail can select stock from the four.
- Carrier commitment schedules must be signed by 30 September, including units by finish and lock dates.
- Moss is the lead finish in Korea and Japan for local assets; Ink is the global lead for product hero images and hands-on units.
- For creative assets, the finish used for hands-on sessions will match the hero finish in global visuals: likely Ink, with some Chalk for fold-demo photos.

- The launch setup web page for the QR code on packaging must be created.
