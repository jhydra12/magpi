## What changed

The team reversed a major material decision in August: they initially switched to ultra-thin glass for the device’s outer layer, but after test failures, changed back to polymer the same day. This reversal cost $37,000 in one-time charges but ultimately saved about $35,000 in capital expenditures and avoided schedule delays. In inventory planning, they clarified the tier split and colourway mix for 180,000 devices, outlining the approach for handling potential mix errors through recutting the tier 2 build based on actual sales data from tier 1.

## What was decided

They confirmed that the device is working, with supporting test data. The unit cost is $1,140 and the launch price remains $1,899, ensuring a 40% gross margin. It was decided that the main board pack messaging would focus on these points. For inventory, tier 1 will launch first in five key markets, with tier 2 following six weeks later in four additional markets. France’s tier 2 inventory will be prioritized and fully stocked before launch to avoid stranded inventory, while Australia may absorb extra holding costs if needed. Real order data from tier 1 will be used to recut the colour mix for tier 2, with Moss finish as the main buffer for mix adjustments.
