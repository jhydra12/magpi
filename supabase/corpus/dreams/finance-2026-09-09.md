## What changed

The team approved the immediate purchase of the delayed “board to panel 4 flex” connector from a broker at four times the list price (costing $2,240 for 200 units, though only 60 are needed) to avoid delaying the 50-unit pilot build. The colourway plan was updated to base coating runs on “pieces” not just finished devices—this rebasing did not change the total colour cost per unit, which remains at $2.48. Colourway modelling now consistently assumes four production finishes rather than the earlier plan for six.

## What was decided

- The connector from the broker will be bought at an increased price for the pilot to preserve the build schedule, despite the cost.
- The connector’s supply issue is to be second-sourced for production, with acknowledgement that this should have happened earlier.
- The “first article” build date for the pilot is unaffected by the connector; the risk only impacts the full production run, and the team will know by the 18th if the broker shipment arrives in time.
- Colourway setup is locked to four finishes, which fits anodising and coating constraints within the available production window, lowering total colour costs from the six-finishes scenario.
