## What changed

The team clarified the specific questions the Field segment asks about durability, emphasizing that previous claims addressed the wrong concerns. They established a detailed claim list for what can and cannot be said about durability, specifying that all language needs Sam's signoff on wording, not just the claim itself. The segment is now handled with directness, prioritizing truth over marketing spin, and new guidance reflects that. The messaging FAQ and terminology guidance were updated, and the recent change from glass to a polymer outer layer is now incorporated into answers and claims.

## What was decided

- Only say a claim once Sam has signed off on the exact wording.
- Claims about cycle specification (200,000 folds) and related lifetime (approx. two years) can be made, as long as the caveats travel with the claim.
- State plainly: the outer layer is polymer, not glass, and it creases less but is more prone to scratches than glass.
- Do not volunteer comparative numbers or competitor information.
- Descriptions of the used unit are important for the Field segment, but such a unit does not yet exist—photographing a used unit is proposed but not the note-taker's decision to make.
- Don’t use marketing adjectives or highlight durability in headlines for the Field segment.
- A set of forbidden and approved phrases for FAQ and messaging was finalized.
